Resources & Tools

Practical tools for Australians — check if your data has been breached, learn from the best, and connect with the right organisations.

Has your email been in a data breach?

HaveIBeenPwned, run by Australian security researcher Troy Hunt, checks your email address against billions of leaked records. It's free and takes ten seconds. The check happens on their site, so your email is never sent to pseudosec.

Check on haveibeenpwned.com

If you're in a breach: change that password (and anywhere you reused it), turn on multi-factor authentication, and be extra wary of emails that mention the breached service.

Report a Scam or Cyber Incident

ACSC Report a Cyber Incident
Australian Signals Directorate's reporting portal for cyber incidents affecting Australian organisations or individuals.
cyber.gov.au/report
ScamWatch — Report a Scam
ACCC's ScamWatch collects reports from Australians. Your report helps protect others and informs national scam statistics.
scamwatch.gov.au
IDCARE — Identity Theft Support
Australia and New Zealand's national identity & cyber support service. Free case managers help you respond to identity theft.
idcare.org · 1800 595 160
ACSC Cyber Security Hotline
24/7 hotline for Australians needing cyber security assistance. Free, confidential, government-run.
1300 CYBER1 (1300 292 371)
National Anti-Scam Centre
Launched in 2023, the NASC disrupts scam operations and coordinates responses across government and industry. Part of the ACCC — your report directly feeds national disruption efforts.
accc.gov.au/nasc

Security Tools

HaveIBeenPwned
Check if your email or phone number has appeared in a data breach. Free, run by Australian Troy Hunt (QLD). Trusted globally.
Free · Australian-run
VirusTotal
Analyse suspicious files, URLs, and email attachments using 70+ antivirus engines simultaneously. Free for basic use.
Free
OSINT Framework
Interactive map of open-source intelligence tools. Useful for understanding what information about you or your organisation is publicly visible.
Free
CISA KEV Catalog
CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog — the definitive list of vulnerabilities being actively used in attacks right now.
Free · Authoritative
Shodan
Search engine for internet-connected devices. Security professionals use it to understand what their organisation exposes to the internet.
Free basic · Intermediate
MXToolbox
Check email security configuration — SPF, DKIM, DMARC. Find out if your domain is properly protected against email spoofing.
Free

Capture the Flag & Practice

Hands-on platforms for building real cyber security skills — from complete beginners to advanced practitioners. CTF (Capture the Flag) challenges let you practice in legal, safe environments.

TryHackMe
Browser-based labs covering pentesting, blue teaming, and security fundamentals. Guided learning paths — great for beginners. No setup required.
Free tier · Beginner friendly
Hack The Box
Industry-standard practice platform with realistic machines, Sherlocks (blue team investigations), and enterprise training tracks.
Free tier · Intermediate–Advanced
picoCTF
Beginner-friendly CTF platform from Carnegie Mellon University. Covers cryptography, forensics, web, and binary exploitation. Fully free.
Free · Beginner
CTFtime
Global calendar of upcoming CTF competitions. Find events by difficulty and format — a good way to practise against real challenges with a team.
Free · All levels
CyberDefenders
Blue team focused labs: incident response, threat hunting, malware analysis, and SIEM investigations. Realistic SOC analyst scenarios.
Free tier · Blue team · Intermediate
Malware Traffic Analysis
Free archive of real-world packet captures (PCAPs) for Wireshark practice. Used by security analysts to study malware behaviour and C2 traffic patterns.
Free · Wireshark · Network analysis

Australian Organisations

Australian Signals Directorate (ACSC)
Australia's national cyber security authority. Advisories, guidance, incident reporting, and the Essential Eight framework.
Office of the Australian Information Commissioner
Oversight of Australia's Privacy Act and Notifiable Data Breaches scheme. Report and check breach notifications here.
ACCC — Scams & Fraud
Australia's consumer watchdog. Tracks scam trends, publishes Scamwatch data, and takes enforcement action against operators.
eSafety Commissioner
Australia's independent regulator for online safety. Handles complaints about cyberbullying, image-based abuse, and illegal online content. Provides support resources for individuals and educators.
Essential Eight Maturity Model
ASD's framework for prioritising cyber security controls. Includes maturity level guidance and assessment tools for Australian businesses and government agencies. Being evolved into ASD's new Essentials series.
